Safeguarding and Data

Your safety comes first.

Every Ayana educator is vetted and trained in child safeguarding. Classes follow clear safeguarding practices, and we have a named safeguarding lead whom families can contact directly. We collect only what we need, protect it carefully, and never sell it.
