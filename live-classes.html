Live Classes

Classes where children think, speak and build.

Our live classes are led by experienced educators who know their subjects and know children. In English, students build confident communication. In STEM, they learn to ask better questions and test ideas. In Debate, they learn to reason, listen and speak with clarity.

Every learner is known by name, and parents receive regular progress updates, so you always see where your child is heading.

Beyond the screen

Learning doesn't end when the class does.

We believe children learn best when they can touch, make and test things. Alongside live lessons, students take part in hands-on activities that carry into everyday life: experiments at the kitchen table, building, observing, discussing. It is a deliberate balance between screen time and the real world.
