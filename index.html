What Ayana is

A learning community designed for every stage of growth

Ayana is a global learning community for curious individuals. Our teachers lead live English, STEM and Debate classes, and our pathways give families a clear route from first lessons to selective universities and meaningful careers. Learning doesn't stop when the screen goes off. It carries into projects, challenges and conversations at home.

Learn live

Small, teacher-led classes in English, STEM and Debate.

Grow with direction

Pathways that give every stage a purpose and a next step.

Belong to something bigger

International English Exams

IELTS, TOEFL and other recognised English proficiency examinations. Preparation in academic English, speaking, listening, reading and writing.

University Preparation

Academic readiness, university admissions guidance, application support and preparation for international higher education pathways.

Professional & Corporate English

Business communication, executive English, professional writing, presentation skills and workplace language development.

Global community and challenges

Learn alongside the world.

Ayana students belong to a community that spans countries and cultures. Through international STEM challenges, they collaborate with peers they would never otherwise meet, solve real problems, and learn that good ideas come from everywhere.

Access and scholarships

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity should be too.

Scholarship seats for students from under-resourced backgrounds are built into Ayana, not added as an afterthought. Every cohort includes learners who are here because of their potential, not their circumstances.

Responsible AI

Technology with purpose, led by teachers.

We use AI thoughtfully, never for its own sake. Educators lead every class. We do not use your data. And we keep a deliberate balance with off-screen learning. We don't chase trends. We ask whether a tool genuinely helps a student learn, and only then do we use it.

Spotlight

A closer look at a few of our Ayana team members.

Angie

Lead Teacher

An accomplished educator with over 20 years of experience spanning preschool to high school, ESL and curriculum development. Former Head of School with extensive leadership experience.

With over 20 years of experience in education, Angela has worked across preschool, primary and high school environments, language schools and ESL programs. Her career spans classroom teaching, educational administration, curriculum development and school leadership, including three years as Head of School. She has also held key positions as Acting Head, Head Teacher, Curriculum Coordinator and Head of Admissions, giving her a comprehensive understanding of school operations and academic management.

Angela believes that every learner is unique, with individual strengths, needs and ways of learning. Her teaching philosophy centres on understanding each student, recognising their potential and adapting the learning experience to their interests and goals. She sees education as more than delivering lessons; it is about guiding, encouraging and equipping learners to grow in confidence and achieve their potential.

Anthony, FRSA, FRGS, FRAS

Senior Advisor

A Stanford and Yale-trained learning architect, Harvard instructional staff member and Oxford keynote speaker, 12-time Stevie Award winner and three-time national champion debate coach.

Founder and CEO of Elite College Hacker, he has pioneered elite admissions strategies, earned prestigious international honours and transformed access to Ivy League education.

Anthony Berryhill is a systems architect for learning design working at the intersection of education, access and technology. He is Founder and CEO of Elite College Hacker, where his proprietary ACCESS Code Method makes the hidden curriculum of elite university admissions visible and teachable. In 2026, the method achieved a 94% client success rate, 100% college persistence and generated over $1.6 million in merit aid, from undergraduate through PhD level.

Previously, Anthony served as Executive Officer and VP of Learning & Development at PIMCO, designing learning systems at enterprise scale. A three-time national champion debate coach, he has spent over 25 years developing advanced communication skills among marginalised students through his Debate Access Project. He also serves on Harvard's instructional staff for Negotiation and as a judge for QS Reimagine Education, the global awards recognising innovation in teaching and learning.

Stanford and Yale-trained, and originally from New Orleans' Lower 9th Ward, Anthony is an international speaker on AI and the future of learning. He most recently delivered the keynote address at the 18th World Leaders Summit at the University of Oxford on The Hidden Curriculum. His distinctions include 12 Stevie Awards, the CEO Clubs Network America 250 Legacy Award, IAOTP's Top Education Changemaker of the Year and a Hermes Creative Platinum Award. He holds three UK Royal Fellowships (FRSA, FRGS and FRAS), alongside PMP and Six Sigma Black Belt certifications.
