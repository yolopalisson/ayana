What Ayana is

A learning community built around your child

Ayana is a global learning community for curious children. Our teachers lead live English, STEM and Debate classes, and our pathways give families a clear route from first lessons to selective universities and meaningful careers. Learning doesn't stop when the screen goes off. It carries into projects, challenges and conversations at home.

Learn live

Small, teacher-led classes in English, STEM and Debate.

Grow with direction

Pathways that give every stage a purpose and a next step.

Belong to something bigger

The ladder

A clear path from curiosity to career

Strong grades are not enough on their own. Selective universities and careers look for depth, initiative and a story. Our pathways help students build all three, step by step.

Core

A strong foundation in English, STEM and Debate, with a growing portfolio of work.

Pathways

Guided direction. Students explore interests, take on bigger challenges and start shaping their university and career goals.

Elite

A small, highly selective programme for students aiming at the world's most competitive universities. Places are limited and by application.


As students grow, they can mentor younger learners and later join our alumni community. Families stay with us because the path keeps opening up.

Beyond the screen

Learning doesn't end when the class does.

We believe children learn best when they can touch, make and test things. Alongside live lessons, students take part in hands-on activities that carry into everyday life: experiments at the kitchen table, building, observing, discussing. It is a deliberate balance between screen time and the real world.

Global community and challenges

Learn alongside the world.

Ayana students belong to a community that spans countries and cultures. Through international STEM challenges, they collaborate with peers they would never otherwise meet, solve real problems, and learn that good ideas come from everywhere.

Access and scholarships

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity should be too.

Scholarship seats for students from under-resourced backgrounds are built into Ayana, not added as an afterthought. Every cohort includes learners who are here because of their potential, not their circumstances.

Responsible AI

Technology with purpose, led by teachers.

We use AI thoughtfully, never for its own sake. Educators lead every class. Children's data is protected. And we keep a deliberate balance with off-screen learning. We don't chase trends. We ask whether a tool genuinely helps a child learn, and only then do we use it.
