Our Story and Founders

Teachers first. Builders second.

Ayana began with a simple frustration: too many people get either good teaching or good direction, rarely both. We are educators and industry professionals who wanted to build the thing we wished existed.

Learn on your terms

Your life doesn't run on a subscription.
Neither should your learning.

No long-term subscriptions. No pressure to squeeze your life around a lesson schedule. Learn when it works for you, and take your time putting your lessons to use.

Your ambitions. Your schedule. Your pace.

Explore flexible learning

  • Why should the lessons you've paid for expire?

    You paid for the lessons. Life got busy. Why should you lose them?

  • Confident enough to let you choose.

    No long-term contracts. No monthly subscriptions. We'd rather earn your continued trust than require your continued commitment.

  • Built for real life

    Busy month at work? Planning a trip? Life happens. Your learning should accommodate that.

Responsible AI

Technology with purpose, led by teachers.

We use AI thoughtfully, never for its own sake. Educators lead every class. Children's data is protected. And we keep a deliberate balance with off-screen learning. We don't chase trends. We ask whether a tool genuinely helps a child learn, and only then do we use it.

Partners and Schools

Let's build something together.

We work with schools, institutions and organisations who share our belief that education should be rigorous, global and humane. Whether you want to offer Ayana programmes to your students, collaborate on a challenge, or support access, we'd like to hear from you.
